# Code of Conduct

This project is small, and its tone is set by whoever shows up. So here is what
is expected, in plain terms, of everyone taking part — issues, pull requests,
discussions and reviews alike.

## What is expected

**Assume the other person is trying.** Someone filing a confused bug report is
not wasting your time; they are telling you the product confused them. Someone
whose pull request misses the point has still spent their evening on your
project.

**Argue about the code, not the person.** "This will break on MySQL because it
quotes identifiers differently" is a review. "Did you even test this?" is not.
The first one gets the bug fixed.

**Say what you mean, without dressing it up or sharpening it.** Direct is fine.
Curt is fine. Contemptuous is not, and the difference is never as subtle as the
person being contemptuous believes.

**Newcomers get patience.** Every maintainer was once the person who could not
get the dev server to start. If a question has been asked before, link the
answer instead of sighing at it — and if it keeps being asked, that is a
documentation bug, not a people problem.

**Take no for an answer.** Not every feature belongs in the project, and not
every review comment has to be obeyed. Both of those are decisions someone is
allowed to make, and re-litigating one at length is its own kind of rudeness.

## What is not

Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments. Discrimination on any grounds,
including gender, sexuality, disability, appearance, ethnicity, religion or
age. Sexualised language or attention. Publishing someone's private
information. Sustained disruption of discussions. Continuing any of the above
after being asked to stop.

None of this becomes acceptable because it was phrased as a joke, or because the
person it was aimed at "should be able to take it".

## Scope

This applies everywhere the project lives: the repository, its issues and
discussions, pull requests and reviews, and any space where someone is
representing Open Report.

## If something goes wrong

Report it to **support@openreport.io**. You will get an answer, what you send
will be treated as confidential, and you will not be asked to argue your case in
public.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close discussions, or block accounts,
proportionately to what happened. The first step is usually a private word, and
that is usually the last step too.

## A note on good faith

This document exists to make a small number of situations easier to handle, not
to be quoted at people. Nobody needs to read it to behave well, and nobody has
ever behaved badly because a document failed to forbid it precisely enough.
